AI Policy

Generative AI is bad technology that’s burning up our resources, polluting our planet, converting or library of an internet into a store, and turning it’s users dumb. Ky and I don’t use it in our writing and art, and we beg you not to either.

Creating is what makes us human.

But I can write a book in a fraction of the time if I use AI! I can create a picture without spending years learning how to draw! Well, sure you can. And it takes far longer to run 26.2 miles than it does to drive it, but you haven’t exactly run a marathon if you do it in your car. If you want the achievement, if you want to tell your story, then you really need to do it yourself.
