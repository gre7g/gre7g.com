AI Policy

Generative AI is bad technology that’s burning up our resources, polluting our planet, converting or library of an internet into a store, and turning it’s users dumb. Ky and I don’t use it in our writing and art, and we beg you not to either.

Creating is what makes us human.

But I can write a book in a fraction of the time if I use AI! I can create a picture without spending years learning how to draw! Well, sure you can. And it takes far longer to run 26.2 miles than it does to drive it, but you haven’t exactly run a marathon if you do it in your car. If you want the achievement, if you want to tell your story, then you really need to do it yourself.

Privacy Policy

Does anyone read these? I doubt they do, but they must be important since everyone has one. I'm not stealing one from another site, and I'm not hiring a lawyer, so I'll just put the policy in my own words.

Your privacy is important to me.

If you visit my site, create an account, and/or purchase products from me, my server will get some of your personal information which could include your name, email address, and shipping address. I collect these so I can do things like mail products to you or provide digital downloads to you.

Servers can collect a whole bunch of other information such as IP addresses, digital fingerprints, and tracking cookies to learn more about you like your race, income level, and even sexual orientation. I have no need for that sort of information, so my server code does not collect it.

My code does store a tracking cookie in your webserver, so I can keep a shopping cart for you without making you sign in. If you put something in your cart today and come back again tomorrow, you'll still see that item in your cart waiting for you. I don't share that tracking cookie with anyone, and none of the big corporations can access that data.

I will not sell your personal information. I don't even know how to do that, honestly, and I hate being spammed as much as you do, so no, I won't contribute to that mess.

Technically, I'd have to reveal your personal information if compelled by a subpoena, but that seems vanishingly improbable. I started running websites back in the late 90’s, and thus far, that’s never happened. I suppose if you check back and see that statement omitted from this page, then you’ll know that it actually happened!

Who will I share your information with? Just the entities you might expect, really. If I have to mail you something, then your name and address are going on a package. If I need to give you a tracking code for your package, then it'll go through your PayPal account. For regular accounting, Rafe (my voice actor) will only receive anonymized IDs instead of your name. But otherwise, I keep your personal information secret unless you tell me I can share it.

Will I spam your email address? I don't do that currently and don't plan to start, but it's entirely possible that I might have to contact you in the future about something important, so I'm hesitant to say never.

If you have a specific question, want to receive a copy of your data, or delete it from my server, please drop the request on my contact form and be sure to include a valid e-mail address so I can reply.

Thanks!
